Italy · Florence

A ZTL fine from Florence

Florence produces more confused foreign drivers than any other Italian city, and almost always for the same reason: the hotel was supposed to register the plate, and something went wrong between reception and the city's system.

What “the hotel will sort it” actually means

A hotel inside the zone can register a guest's plate so the entry is treated as authorised. It is not automatic and it is not instant. Someone at reception has to enter your plate, within the window the city allows, and they have to get the plate right.

Three things go wrong routinely: nobody enters it, it is entered after the window closes, or a digit is wrong - and a wrong digit means both you and the owner of the real plate have a problem.

The appeal lives or dies on one email If you sent your plate to the hotel in writing and have their reply, hotel-failure appeals succeed in roughly a third of cases. If the exchange happened at the desk with nothing in writing, there is no ground here, and appealing will cost you more than paying.

If you have the email

Keep the whole thread, including the timestamps, and the booking confirmation showing the dates. Those three documents together are the appeal. Add the rental agreement if the car was hired, because it proves which plate was actually yours.

If you do not

Check the letter for a factual error instead - plate, date, time, make of car. Any of those is a far stronger ground than the hotel story. If everything on the letter is correct, pay inside the first five days and take the 30% off.

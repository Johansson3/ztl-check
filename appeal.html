How and where to send it You have 60 days from the date of notification. Send it by registered post with return receipt to the Prefecture of the province that issued the fine, or hand it to the office of the officer who issued it — both routes are allowed. Keep the receipt.
What happens if the Prefect rejects it The Prefect issues an order for a sum of not less than double the statutory minimum for the violation, plus costs, payable within 30 days. That is the risk you are taking. The alternative route — the Justice of the Peace — runs 30 days from notification, or 60 days if you live outside Italy, and carries a court fee.
Read it before you sign This is a template filled with what you typed. Check that the fine number, the plate, the comune and the dates match your letter exactly.

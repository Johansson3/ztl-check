Italy · Rome

A ZTL fine from Rome

Rome is the city where people most often get more than one fine from a single trip, because it does not have one restricted zone. It has several, next to each other, each with its own boundary and its own active hours.

Why one weekend produces three letters

Crossing from one part of the centre to another can mean leaving one zone and entering another, and every entry is read separately. A driver circling to find a hotel entrance can trigger several in under an hour. If you have received more than one letter with different file numbers, that is why — and each one has its own deadline.

Check each letter separately Different file numbers mean different clocks. Running them through the check one at a time is the only way to avoid losing a discount on one while dealing with another.

The hotel question

Hotels inside the zone can register a guest's plate. In practice this depends entirely on the hotel doing it, and doing it in time. If you gave reception your plate and they confirmed it in writing, you have something worth appealing with. If the arrangement was verbal, you do not.

What to do now

Work out which of the three documents you are holding, because the answer is different for each. The rental company's charge is not the fine. A collection agency notice cannot be appealed yet. Only the official letter from the city starts your 60 days.
